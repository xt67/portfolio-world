Pune Air Quality Sentinel · Rayan Rahman

Following a forecast back to its source

Choose a Pune location to explore weather and air-quality forecasts. The dashboard uses provider data, keeps a short cache, and identifies stale responses when a fresh request fails.

Nearby locations can share a regional model grid. Reading the displayed AQI therefore requires both the index convention and the source of the estimate.

What has been checked

  • Fetches current weather and air-quality forecasts for ten Pune locations.
  • Caches responses and explicitly labels stale or unavailable data.
  • Shows provider forecasts using US EPA AQI, not Indian CPCB AQI or project-trained model predictions.

CAMS data is regional modelling, not measured station observations. Nearby locations can share a forecast grid.
