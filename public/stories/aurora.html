AURORA · Rayan Rahman

Testing synthetic augmentation

AURORA asks whether adding synthetic images can help a classifier learn from a limited set of TB-labelled chest X-rays. A real-only baseline provides a reference for the augmentation comparisons.

The selection protocol removes images involved in flagged candidate pairs from the proposed subset. The flags identify candidates for review. The research does not establish patient independence or clinical readiness.

What has been checked

  • Compares real-only training, oversampling, conventional augmentation and DCGAN-generated images.
  • A two-epoch feasibility run completed using 52 TB training images. Publication experiments and conclusions remain pending.
  • The proposed subset retains 590 of 800 NLM images after automated candidate-pair exclusions. Patient independence and a duplicate-free subset are not claimed.

Research prototype only. Synthetic images do not guarantee privacy, diagnostic validity or better performance. No clinical deployment is validated.
