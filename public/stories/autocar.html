AutoCar-Sim · Rayan Rahman

From recorded frames to a steering decision

A drive produces a sequence of related camera frames. RGB and depth observations need to correspond to the same simulation step before they can describe one driving decision.

Holding out complete driving sessions gives the evaluator a stronger test than shuffling neighboring frames. The portfolio driving terminal explores those decisions through a separate browser simulation.

What has been checked

  • Pairs RGB and depth sensor frames and records session provenance.
  • Separates related driving samples during evaluation and records held-out sample identities.
  • Compares learned controllers in repeated CARLA scenarios. The portfolio terminal is a separate, lightweight browser experience.

Browser gameplay is an illustrative simulation. It does not run CARLA, collect research data or train a deployed autonomous-driving model.
